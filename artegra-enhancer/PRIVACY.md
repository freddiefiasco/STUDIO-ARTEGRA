# Privacy

The app processes images locally and does not automatically upload images, logs or feedback. There is no analytics service or automatic update check.

The host and its service communicate through authenticated loopback connections. Worker connections also bind to loopback. Model and runtime setup are separate actions initiated by the user; publishers receive the normal network information associated with those downloads.

Local logs can contain filenames and other information about jobs. Review logs and remove personal paths before attaching them to a public issue. Never attach credentials, access tokens or client images unless you have permission to share them.
